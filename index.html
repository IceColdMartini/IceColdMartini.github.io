Lecturer of Computer Science & Engineering

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at United International University (UIU) and previously at the Military Institute of Science & Technology (MIST). I am currently a prospective Research-based Master's student with an intention to pursue a PhD and Postdoctoral research.

My research fundamentally explores the intersection of Model Compression, LLM Reasoning, and ClinicalAI. I am passionate about developing end-device deployable, privacy-preserving Small Language Models (SLMs) for high-stakes environments like surgical automation and diagnostics. My long-term vision is to bridge human-AI collaboration to facilitate precise, real-time medical reasoning at the edge.

Research Focus

Current Questions Driving My Work:

  • Can we achieve pure multimodality for Small Language Models (SLMs)? If yes, how can we develop adaptive architectures preserving diagnostic-critical features?
  • How can we articulate a more optimized data-efficient flow to train context-rich student Small-VLMs from proprietary LLMs/VLMs?
  • How can we ensure end-device deployable, privacy-preserving SLM development (e.g., via Differential Privacy) in ClinicalAI settings for edge-computing?
